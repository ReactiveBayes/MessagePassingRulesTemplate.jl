Where these rules run

The ReactiveMP engine runs these rules on a factor graph, which RxInfer builds from a model. The examples here call the rules directly.

Overview

Use the node for an observation or a latent variable with a normal distribution whose variance is known. Its mean can be another variable of the model, observed or not.

Definition

\[f(\mathrm{out}, \mu, v) = \mathcal{N}(\mathrm{out} \mid \mu, v)\]

Algorithm

The node uses DefaultAlgorithm, which has no parameters, so a model names none. It declares no dependencies of its own: its rules take what the default scheme delivers.

  • Belief propagation, towards out from the messages on μ and v, and towards μ from the messages on out and v: the variances add. The log scale is 0.
  • Variational message passing, towards out from the marginals q(μ) and q(v), and towards μ from q(out) and q(v): only the mean of the other marginal counts.
  • The average energy, from q(out), q(μ) and q(v).

Limitations

  • The variance must be a PointMass: there are no rules for a random variance.
  • Univariate only.
  • The variational rules declare no log scale.

API

ExampleMessagePassingRules.ExampleNormal — Type
ExampleNormal(μ, v)

A normal distribution over out with mean μ and variance v, as a stochastic factor node: f(out, μ, v) = N(out | μ, v). Called with its parameters, it is that distribution, a NormalMeanVariance.

Interfaces

  1. out: the variable the distribution is over.
  2. μ: the mean, also named mean.
  3. v: the variance, a PointMass.

Rules

  • belief propagation, towards out from m(μ) and m(v), and towards μ from m(out) and m(v): the variances add, with log scale 0;
  • variational message passing, towards out from q(μ) and q(v), and towards μ from q(out) and q(v);
  • the average energy, from q(out), q(μ) and q(v).

The variance must be positive; every rule checks it.

See also ExampleMessagePassingRules.
